# SmartTask bug hunt, 6 November 2026

Final report. Unless a bug says otherwise, tests ran with the clock at Friday 6 November 2026, Israel time (Asia/Jerusalem).

## 1. Summary

**75 confirmed bugs: 3 high, 28 medium, 44 low.**
Also: 7 plausible but not demonstrated, 0 disputed, 6 rejected.

The three that matter most:

1. **R1-1.** Putting `#project`, `@tag`, `!!` or a word like `urgent` before a date mangles the saved title. `#home pay rent friday` is saved as `pay r iday`. This happens in English and in Hebrew.
2. **R1-2.** A due date with a time (Notion's "Include time") drops the task out of Today, Overdue and the badges. Upcoming lists it under "Invalid Date".
3. **R1-3.** If you paste a Notion link whose title ends in 0-9 or a-f (Home, Life, DB, 2026), the app reads the wrong id. The error message then tells you to re-share the database, which does not help.

How the hunt ran:

- 3 rounds and 25 hunters, each with its own area (dates, parser, Notion, Gemini, storage, voice, security, accessibility, Android behaviour, docs accuracy and more). Section 7 has the full list.
- A verifier tried to reproduce every finding against the real code:
  - 60 in the built app, in headless Chromium emulating a Pixel 7 with a Hebrew locale and Israel time zone. Notion and Gemini were replaced by fakes at the network layer.
  - 14 with Node scripts that import the real source files.
  - 1 by running the test suite.
- An independent skeptic then tried to refute the three high-severity bugs. All three were upheld.
- No hunter, verifier, skeptic or critic run failed.
- Round 1 found 52 of the confirmed bugs, round 2 found 15 and round 3 found 8.

Bugs that share a cause and are worth fixing together:

- **How the parser cuts text** (`parse.ts`): R1-1, R1-10, R1-19. Title tidying: R1-44, R2-2.
- **Date matching** (`parse.ts`): R1-4, R1-9, R1-20, R1-21, R1-22.
- **Notion id from a link:** R1-3 (app) and R2-5 (scripts). Same flaw, two copies of the code.
- **Board acting on an old copy of the list:** R1-5, R1-6, R1-7, R3-5. Also plausible R3-6.
- **Board unmounted while work is still running:** R1-26, R3-4, R3-1.
- **Wrong Notion error messages:** R1-36, R3-3, R2-14, R2-13, R1-49.
- **UTC date used instead of the local date:** R1-34, R2-16.
- **Category identity (letter case, length, invisible marks):** R2-1, R2-3, R3-9.

### High

#### R1-1. A token before a date corrupts the saved title

`src/lib/parse.ts:263`. Reproduced in the built app. **Skeptic: reviewed, upheld.**

**What goes wrong.** You type a priority marker, `#project`, `@tag` or a priority word (`urgent`, `important`, `asap`) before the date. The app cuts the date from the wrong place: letters disappear from real words and part of the date word stays. The due date itself is correct. The categorizer (rules or Gemini) sees the damaged title, so the category can change too. The preview shows only chips, never the title, so you cannot catch it before tapping Add.

**Trigger.** Type and Add:

| Input | Saved title |
|---|---|
| `#home pay rent friday` | `pay r iday` |
| `!! call the bank tomorrow` | `call the bank w` |
| `urgent call bank tomorrow` | `call orrow` |
| `call mom @family tomorrow` | `call mom ow` |
| `#home @bills pay rent next monday` | `xt monday` |
| `#בית לשלם חשבון 25/12` | `לשלם חשב /12` |
| `!! לשלם ארנונה 15/11` | `לשלם ארנונה 1` |
| `important meeting tomorrow` | `tomorrow` |

`urgent call the bank tomorrow` is also filed under Personal instead of Finance, because "bank" was cut.

**Evidence.**
- A Node probe on the real `parseTask`/`captureDraft` (`r1-verify-parser0/probe.ts`) gave every title above.
- In the built app, localStorage held `{title:'pay r iday'}` and `{title:'call the orrow', category:'Personal'}`. With Gemini on, Gemini received the damaged `לשלם ארנונה 1`.
- Controls: the same tokens placed after the date, or a single `!`, give clean titles.
- The 131 existing tests pass. The only test with tokens puts them after the date.

**Cause.** Line 263 builds a "masked" copy of the text by replacing each token with one space, so the copy is shorter than the original. The date is found by its position in the shorter copy, and then that position is cut from the original. Each token before the date shifts the cut by (token length − 1). The comment at lines 261-262 says the positions line up. They do not.

**Fix direction.** Mask with filler of the same length (`' '.repeat(end - start)`) so positions match. Merge overlapping spans before cutting. Add tests with tokens before the date. Fixing this together with R1-10 and R1-19 makes sense.

**Skeptic.** Reproduced every case with its own script (`r1-refute-parser0/p.ts`). Confirmed that both stores save the damaged title, and that Gemini cannot repair it: `capture.ts` uses Gemini's title only when the parser found no date. Not refuted.

#### R1-2. A due date with a time breaks Today, Overdue and Upcoming

`src/lib/views.ts:45`. Reproduced in the built app. **Skeptic: reviewed, upheld.**

**What goes wrong.** When "Include time" is on in Notion, Notion returns the due date as, for example, `2026-11-06T17:00:00.000+02:00`. The app stores it as is, and the views then cannot work out how many days away it is. The effects:
- The task is never overdue or due today, so it drops out of Today, Overdue, Focus urgency and the badge counts.
- A timed task due today shows in Upcoming under an "Invalid Date" heading.
- A timed task on the last day of the Upcoming window is dropped.
- Chips show the raw timestamp.

Restoring a backup with a timed due date does the same.

**Trigger.** Notion mode, 12:00 on 6 Nov. Notion has three open tasks:
- due `2026-11-06T14:00:00.000+02:00` (today)
- due `2026-11-05T09:00:00.000+02:00` (Urgent, overdue)
- due `2026-11-06` (no time)

Open Today, then Upcoming.

**Expected.** Today badge 3, with the overdue task under Overdue.

**Actual.**
- The Today badge is 1 and shows only the task without a time.
- The overdue task appears nowhere.
- Upcoming shows an "INVALID DATE" group, and the chip reads `2026-11-06T14:00:00.000+02:00`.
- The counts report overdue 0.

**Evidence.**
- Node through the real `pageToTask` and views (`r1-verify-dates2/r1-2.ts`): `daysUntilDue` is null for every timed due date.
- Built app with mocked Notion: tabs read "Today 1", "All 3". Screenshots: `r1-verify-dates2/r1-2-Today.png` and `r1-2-Upcoming.png`.

**Cause.**
- `readDate` (`notion-mapping.ts:72-77`) passes Notion's value through unchanged.
- `sanitizeStoredTask` (`local-store.ts:51`) only checks that the value starts with YYYY-MM-DD.
- `views.ts:45` and `:99` append `T00:00:00`, which gives an invalid date.
- The Upcoming filter (`views.ts:140`) compares raw strings.
- `dueLabel` (`Chips.tsx:56`) prints the raw string.

**Fix direction.** Convert to the local calendar date when reading from Notion and when restoring. Keep date-only values as they are. Convert datetimes to the local date rather than slicing the first 10 characters, because a UTC time can fall on a different local day. Views could also slice to 10 characters as a safety net.

**Skeptic.** Re-ran it and got the same output. Confirmed that real Notion returns a full datetime when "Include time" is set, and that PLAN.md says you may edit tasks in Notion. Caveats: the backup variant needs a hand-edited file, and the fix must convert UTC times properly. Not refuted.

#### R1-3. A Notion link whose title ends in a hex character gives the wrong id

`src/lib/config.ts:22` (and `scripts/lib/env.mjs:21`, see R2-5). Reproduced in the built app and in the setup script. **Skeptic: reviewed, upheld.**

**What goes wrong.** To find the id in a pasted Notion link, the app removes every dash and then takes the first 32 hex characters. Notion links look like `Title-Words-<id>`. When the title ends in 0-9 or a-f, those characters join the id, and the result is shifted but still looks valid. Notion answers "not found". The app, and the README, say this almost always means the Connections step is missing, so you re-share a database that is already shared. The only way out is to paste the bare id.

**Trigger.** On the Connect screen, paste
`https://www.notion.so/myworkspace/Tasks-DB-0a8fbd6249a948a7a23a68ee2e74e191?v=…`
Or run setup with `NOTION_PARENT_PAGE_ID=https://www.notion.so/myworkspace/My-Home-<id>`.

**Actual.**
- Tasks-DB gives `db0a8fbd-6249-a948-a7a2-3a68ee2e74e1`.
- Home gives `e0a8fbd6-…`.
- Tasks-2026 gives `20260a8f-…`.
- Life gives `fe0a8fbd-…`.
- An encoded Hebrew title gives `aa0a8fbd-…`.

The banner reads: "Notion cannot find that database. Open it in Notion, then ••• → Connections, and add your integration."

**Evidence.**
- `r1-verify-notion3/ids.mjs` ran the real functions on these inputs.
- In the built app, the stored databaseId was `db0a8fbd-…` and the request was `GET /v1/databases/db0a8fbd-…`.
- `setup-notion.mjs` with a fake fetch sent a shifted `parent.page_id` and printed the Connections advice.
- The repo's test uses `SmartTask-<id>`. It passes only because "k" is not a hex character.

**Cause.** Both functions remove every dash before searching, so the dash that separates title and id is lost.

**Fix direction.** Search only the path (before `?`) for an id that is not touching other hex characters, with or without dashes, and take the last match. Make the same change in `scripts/lib/env.mjs`. Add tests for `Tasks-DB-<id>` and `My-Home-<id>`.

**Skeptic.** Upheld, using the sample URL from Notion's own API docs (`Example-Page-1429989f…` becomes `e1429989-…`). Caveat: Notion's "Copy link" for a full-page database is usually `/workspace/<id>?v=…` with no title, so the in-app Connect path probably fires less often than the verifier claimed. The setup script's parent-page path is hit reliably.

### Medium

#### R1-4. Decimals, prices, versions and times are read as day.month dates

`src/lib/parse.ts:88`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** Numbers like `1.5`, `5.10`, `3.12`, `1/2` or `9.05` are read as day.month. The number is removed from the title and the task gets a due date months away. This beats an explicit "tomorrow" or "מחר" in the same text, and it also beats Gemini's correct date.

**Trigger.** With Gemini on, add `לקנות 1.5 ליטר חלב מחר`. Also:
- `meeting at 9.05 tomorrow`
- `upgrade python 3.12`
- `pay 5.10 for parking`
- `buy 1/2 kg cheese`
- `install node 22.11`

**Actual.**
- `לקנות ליטר חלב מחר`, due 2027-05-01 (chip "1 במאי").
- `meeting at tomorrow`, due 2027-05-09.
- python 3.12: 2026-12-03.
- 5.10: 2027-10-05.
- 1/2: 2027-02-01.
- 22.11: 2026-11-22.

**Evidence.**
- `r1-verify-parser0/probe.ts`.
- `captureDraft` with a faked Gemini that answered correctly: Gemini's due date of 2026-11-07 was discarded.
- The built app with a fake Gemini stored the same results.
- Prices where the second part is over 12 (49.90, 12.50) are not affected.

**Cause.** The day/month pattern accepts any N.M with N ≤ 31 and M ≤ 12, whatever the context. It runs before the relative words, and `capture.ts:53` always prefers the parser's date. The d.m format itself is intended, because Israelis write 25.12.

**Fix direction.** Make a bare N.M stricter: reject it before a unit or noun, or when a relative date word is also present. Do not let a weak day/month match override "tomorrow" or Gemini's date.

#### R1-5. A list read already in flight undoes a newer add, tick or delete

`src/components/TaskBoard.tsx:89`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** A list read can be in flight: the first load, ↻, or the refresh when you return to the app. If you add, tick, delete or change a category during it, the read lands afterwards and replaces the list with the older copy:
- The new card vanishes, although the capture note says it was filed.
- A ticked task reopens.
- A deleted task comes back.

The 20-second cache also stores that old result even though the write had just cleared the cache, so reopening the board within 20 s shows it again without a request. Re-adding the "vanished" task creates a duplicate in Notion.

**Trigger.** Notion mode, with list reads slower than writes (mobile data, a large database):
- (a) Add a task before the first load finishes.
- (b) Return to the app after more than 10 s and tick a task straight away.

Then open ✦ AI and tap done within 20 s.

**Evidence.** `r1-verify-state-ui6/r1-5-read-race.mjs`, using a fake Notion that answers late:
- (a) After the capture the cards were `[call the bank]`. After the load landed they were `[book dentist | renew passport]`, while Notion had "call the bank".
- (b) A ticked task came back as open while Notion had it as Done. After AI settings and done, the query count stayed at 2 and the old list was shown again.
- (c) A deleted task came back.

`cache-race.ts` ran against the real `cache.ts` and showed the in-flight read being cached after `invalidate()`.

**Cause.**
- `load` (lines 85-97) always applies what the read returns.
- `busyRef` (line 260) only stops a refresh from starting during a capture or sort.
- `cached()` in `cache.ts` (lines 27-28) stores the result even if `invalidate()` ran in the meantime.

**Fix direction.** Keep a write counter. Drop a read result if a write happened after the read started. Give cache entries an epoch that `invalidate()` bumps.

#### R1-6. A failed write restores the whole old list and wipes other changes

`src/components/TaskBoard.tsx:141`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** When a tick, delete or category change fails (after a delay, such as a rate limit or a dropped connection), the app restores the entire list as it was when you tapped. Anything else saved in the meantime, like another tick or a new task, disappears from the screen. With two failures, a tick that was never saved can show as done.

**Trigger.** Notion mode. Tick task A while its save is failing (it fails after 2 s). In that window, tick B (succeeds) and add a task (succeeds).

**Actual.** A and B both show as open, and the new task is gone from the list, although both B's tick and the new task are in Notion. In the two-failure variant, A shows as Done although Notion has Todo.

**Evidence.** `r1-verify-state-ui6/r1-6-stale-revert.mjs`: the list went from `task C | pay water bill` to `task C | task B | task A`, while Notion had B Done and "pay water bill" saved.

**Cause.** `const previous = tasks` (lines 131, 150, 195) and `setTasks(previous)` on failure (lines 141, 156, 204).

**Fix direction.** On failure, revert only the failed task with a functional update, and put back a deleted task if it is missing. Or re-read the list after a failure. The banner's retry already re-reads, which softens the impact.

#### R1-7. Sort with Gemini overwrites a category you pick during the run

`src/lib/recategorize.ts:73`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** Sort with Gemini works through the Inbox as it was when you tapped. If you move a queued task by hand during the run, Gemini's answer later replaces your choice on screen, after reload, and in Notion. If you delete a queued task during the run, the whole sort stops with "Stopped early: That task no longer exists on this device. Run it again in a minute to continue."

**Trigger.** Gemini on, 3+ Inbox tasks. Tap ✦ Sort with Gemini, then pick Home for the last queued task. Or delete a queued task.

**Evidence.** `r1-verify-state-ui6/r1-7-sort-overwrite.mjs`.
- Phone-only: right after the pick, storage had Home. After the run it had Errands, and that survived a reload.
- Notion log: `PATCH i3 {Category:Home}` at 40.66 s, then `PATCH i3 {Category:Errands}` at 45.51 s.
- Delete variant: "Moved 2 of 4 out of Inbox. Stopped early: …", and Gemini was still asked about the deleted task.

**Cause.** The targets are computed once (`recategorize.ts:48`) from the list at tap time, and each update is written without checking the task's current state.

**Fix direction.** Before each task, look it up in the current list. Skip it if it is no longer an open Inbox task or no longer exists, instead of stopping.

#### R1-8. Text typed during a save is erased when the save finishes

`src/components/QuickAdd.tsx:74`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** After you tap Add, the box stays editable while the save runs, which can take seconds with Gemini or Notion. When the save finishes, the box is cleared, wiping whatever you typed meanwhile: the next task, or a fix to the first. Because a cleared box normally means "added", the loss is easy to miss.

**Trigger.** Gemini answering after about 3 s. Type `לקנות חלב`, tap Add, and while the button says "Saving…" type `להתקשר לאמא`.

**Actual.** The box shows the new text while saving, then becomes empty. Only the first task is stored.

**Evidence.** `r1-verify-state-ui6/r1-8-typing-during-save.mjs`, in both modes: `input disabled=false`, then `box after save: ""`. Variant: replace the text with `second task` and press Enter. Enter is ignored because Add is disabled, and the text is then lost.

**Cause.** The box is cleared unconditionally after the save. The input is not disabled while saving.

**Fix direction.** Clear only if the box still holds the submitted text (`setText(cur => cur.trim() === trimmed ? '' : cur)`), or make the input read-only while saving.

#### R1-9. A real date is missed after "word number", "number word", a price or a time

`src/lib/parse.ts:155`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** Each date pattern only looks at its first match in the text. If that first match is not a valid date (`buy 2`, `6 eggs`, `12.50`, `8.30`), a real date later in the text is never tried. The task is saved with no date, and the date words stay in the title. With AI off nothing recovers it. With Gemini on, Gemini can fill the gap.

**Trigger (AI off).**

With the category sheet, AI settings or the Connect form open, the Android back gesture leaves the site (in a tab) or closes the installed app, instead of closing the overlay. #### R3-3. A read-only Notion integration is told the database cannot be found

#### R3-4. ⏻ during a sort: the sort keeps using the forgotten token

`src/components/TaskBoard.tsx:216`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** If you tap ⏻ while Sort with Gemini runs, the sort keeps going after the token is forgotten. It sends the remaining saves to Notion with the old token and the remaining tasks to Gemini. Its result is never shown. Phone-only mode keeps writing behind the choice screen.

**Evidence.** `r3-verify-state-ui3/v4-power.mjs`: after ⏻ the stored token was null, yet 3 more saves carried `Bearer ntn_fake_token_123456`, at +1.6, +3.3 and +5.0 s.

#### R3-5. The category sheet uses an old copy of the task, so a tap is ignored

`src/components/TaskBoard.tsx:427`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** The sheet holds the task as it was when you opened it. Suppose a running sort or a refresh changes that task while the sheet is open. The sheet still ticks the old category. Tapping that category just closes the sheet: nothing is saved, and your choice is silently dropped.

**Trigger.** Start Sort, then open the sheet on an Inbox task. The sort files it under Errands, and you tap Inbox to keep it there.

**Evidence.** `r3-verify-state-ui3/v5-picker.mjs`: the card chip said Errands, the sheet showed "Inbox ✓", and tapping Inbox sent no save. Phone-only behaves the same.

**Fix direction.** Store only the task id and look up the live task when rendering and saving.

#### R3-7. The README says a newline starts a Note, but the capture box cannot hold one

`README.md:33`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** The capture box is a single-line input, and the browser turns pasted line breaks into spaces. A two-line paste becomes one long title with no note, and date or priority words from the second line apply to the task. Only ` // ` works for notes, and the in-app hint never mentions it.

**Trigger.** Paste `Renew passport`, a newline, then `last time it took 3 weeks, not urgent, someday ask about the 25/12 slot`.

**Actual.** The preview says "Will save as Inbox Urgent Dec 25". The saved title is the whole text with a word removed, no note, due 2026-12-25, priority Urgent. ("not urgent" becoming Urgent is R1-17. The newline is not its cause.)

**Evidence.** `r3-verify-parser0/parse-newline.ts` and `r3-verify-parser0/paste-e2e.mjs`. Ctrl+V with LF, with CRLF, and keyboard insert all lose the newline. ` // ` works.

**Fix direction.** Use a textarea where Enter submits, or turn the first pasted line break into ` // `. Or drop "a newline" from the README. Either way, mention ` // ` in the hint.

#### R3-9. An invisible mark in a category name makes a duplicate category

`src/lib/types.ts:78`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** Text copied from WhatsApp or other Hebrew sources often carries an invisible right-to-left mark. Typing or pasting `מחקר` with one at the end, when `מחקר` already exists, creates a second category. It looks identical, gets its own Notion option, and splits the All view. "Work" with such a mark becomes a custom category next to the built-in Work.

**Evidence.** `r3-verify-storage4/marks.ts` and `r3-verify-storage4/marks-browser.mjs`. The picker listed `מחקר` twice, All showed two groups, and both were saved.

**Fix direction.** In `normalizeCategoryName`, remove bidi marks, zero-width spaces and BOM anywhere. Trim zero-width joiners only at the ends, and apply Unicode NFC normalisation.

#### R3-11. The Connect screen says "disconnect below", but nothing is below

`src/components/ConnectForm.tsx:118`. Reproduced in the built app.

**What goes wrong.** The "Where your token is kept" box ends: "Use a browser you trust, and disconnect below when you are done on a shared device." It is the last thing on the screen. No control anywhere is called disconnect. The real control is the bare ⏻ in the board header, whose only label is a tooltip that phones never show.

**Evidence.** `r3-verify-security5/r3-11.mjs`: no elements below the box, and no button named disconnect or forget. ⏻ itself works.

**Fix direction.** Change the text to "tap ⏻ at the top of your task list to forget the token". Give ⏻ a label (see R1-56).

## 5. Plausible but not demonstrated

In each of these, part of the problem was shown, but the decisive part depends on a real service or device that was not available here.

**R1-24 (medium if confirmed).** Following the in-app advice to restrict the Gemini key to "this site's address" may block every call.
- Shown: the app sends only the origin (`https://itaigree.github.io/`) as Referer.
- Shown: any "referrer blocked" 403 is reported as "That key cannot use the Gemini API. Create one at aistudio.google.com/apikey."
- Shown: the claim that "a copied key is useless anywhere else" is overstated.
- Not shown: that Google rejects a path pattern such as `…/smartask/*` against an origin-only Referer.
- Needed: a real AI Studio key restricted as advised, used from the live site.

**R1-35 (low).** Any Notion error without a code shows "Could not reach Notion. Check your connection".
- Shown: in the app with a mocked database reply that has no data source.
- Not shown: that real Notion ever sends such a reply. The SDK's API version always includes data sources.
- Needed: a real Notion database that returns none, such as a linked database.

**R1-42 (low).** Sort's pacing (one task every ~1.5 s plus latency, about 20-28 a minute) may exceed free-tier limits.
- Shown: with a fake limit of 15 a minute, a 20-task sort stops at 15.
- Shown: tasks Gemini leaves in Inbox are asked again on every run.
- Not shown: Google's real free-tier limits. ai.google.dev was unreachable; third-party pages say about 5-15 a minute.
- Needed: a real free-tier key and a backlog of 20+ Inbox tasks.

**R1-43 (low).** With AI on, an @tag naming a category does not "win outright" as the README says.
- Shown: the tag rule exists only in the keyword rules, and the prompt passes tags as a hint next to "judge by what the task is about".
- Not shown: what real Gemini answers.
- Needed: real Gemini on `pay the electricity bill @home` (likely Finance, since the prompt's own example says so) and `buy printer ink @work`.

**R1-47 (low).** If dictation is cut off because the page was hidden, the partial text is left with no message and no review hint.
- Shown: with a fake recognizer sending "aborted".
- Not shown: that Android Chrome actually sends "aborted" when you switch apps mid-sentence. Chromium's source suggests it does.
- Needed: a real Android phone. Dictate, switch apps mid-sentence, and come back.

**R2-12 (low).** A double space in a Notion category name is collapsed on read, and the collapsed name is written back.
- Shown: the app writes "Side Project" for an option named "Side  Project".
- Not shown: whether Notion keeps the double space (creating a second option) or normalises it.
- Needed: real Notion.

**R3-6 (low).** Two quick saves to the same task, such as a category then a tick: if the older response arrives last, the screen reverts the newer change. The task reappears as open while Notion has it as Done.
- Shown: with a mock that delivers responses out of order.
- Not shown: real Notion doing that.
- Needed: real Notion over a real mobile connection.

## 6. Rejected

These were checked and ruled out, as intended design or missing features rather than bugs:

- **R2-4. No offline start once the cache expires (no service worker).** Offline mode is out of v1 scope (PLAN.md decision 7), and no data is lost. Worth considering as a feature.
- **R2-8. Hebrew urgency words (דחוף, חשוב) do not set priority.** The README documents English words only, and `!` or `!!` works on Hebrew text. A missing feature.
- **R2-11. The error banner and stale board persist after the connection returns.** This is the documented 10-second refresh throttle, and both retry and ↻ work.
- **R3-8. Phone-only mode keeps yesterday's dates after a night in the background.** Overdue timers fire on resume. The test assumed zero awake time overnight. Real staleness is bounded by the 60-second tick.
- **R3-10. The README's Focus ranking example breaks once the age bonus counts.** It follows the documented weighted formula, and the counterexamples are unusual. At most a README wording point.
- **R3-12. An unknown Notion Status (e.g. Cancelled) is read as Todo.** Status is a documented closed set. Todo is the safe default, and nothing in the app overwrites it.

**What was hunted.** 3 rounds, 25 hunters:

- Notion: notion, sweep-notion, blackbox-notion
- Storage, views and categories: storage, sweep-store-views, blackbox-local, category-identity

- Security: security
- Build and scripts: build, sweep-scripts

- Phone behaviour: offline-pwa, android-lifecycle
- Cross-checks: sibling-sweep (looked for relatives of confirmed bugs), docs-copy-truth (checked README and on-screen text against behaviour)

**How it was checked.**
- The static export was served locally under `/smartask/` and driven by Playwright in headless Chromium: Pixel 7 emulation, he-IL locale, Asia/Jerusalem time zone, the clock usually fixed at Friday 6 Nov 2026.
- Notion and Gemini were replaced at the network layer by fakes that follow the documented API shapes (paging, error codes, page objects). The app's real Notion SDK ran unchanged.
- Node scripts imported the real source with `TZ=Asia/Jerusalem`.
- The existing 131 tests pass on Node 22.23 and 24.22.

**Not verified here:**

- **Real Notion API.** api.notion.com and developers.notion.com are blocked from the sandbox. Affected:
  - whether "Copy link" includes the title (R1-3)
  - case-variant options (R2-3)
  - the option name length limit (R2-1)
  - double spaces (R2-12)
  - the exact 403 body (R3-3, R2-14)
  - response ordering (R3-6)
  - empty data sources (R1-35)
- **Real Gemini.** No real key was used, and every answer was scripted. Affected:
  - how likely the slips in R1-11 and R1-13 are
  - the tag behaviour in R1-43
  - the AI part of R2-2
  - free-tier limits (R1-42) and quota error bodies (R1-41)
  - referrer matching (R1-24)
- **Real Android phone.** No device, microphone or TalkBack.
  - Voice used a scripted fake recognizer (R1-28, R1-46, R1-47, R1-48).
  - TalkBack behaviour was inferred from Chromium's accessibility tree (R1-30, R1-56).
  - Installed-app (WebAPK) back and close behaviour was inferred (R3-1, R3-2).
  - The native select picker was not tested (R1-23).
  - Background tab discards were simulated with a renderer crash.
- **GitHub.** No fresh-repo deploy was run, because that would change state on GitHub (R1-29).
